Build a complete, polished, hackathon-ready web application called:

OPS MIND AI
Tagline: "Turn scattered enterprise information into intelligent action."

CONTEXT:
This is an Enterprise AI hackathon prototype. The goal is to demonstrate how AI can transform fragmented enterprise information into actionable tasks, automated workflows, risk alerts, and decision-ready insights.

IMPORTANT:
Build a FUNCTIONAL prototype, not a static landing page.
Prioritize working user flows and realistic demo data.
The application must run successfully in Replit without requiring complicated external infrastructure.

==================================================
1. CORE PROBLEM
==================================================

Organizations have information scattered across documents, meeting notes, spreadsheets, emails, tasks, and different departments.

Employees spend significant time:
- searching for information
- identifying action items
- following up with teams
- tracking deadlines
- preparing reports
- understanding why decisions were made

This leads to information silos, missed deadlines, operational bottlenecks, and slow decision-making.

OpsMind AI solves this by creating an intelligent enterprise operations layer.
